**Figure 1a-d | Patient-level HRIC alpha-diversity trajectories across
allo-HSCT and trial randomization.** **a,b,** Control patients (\(n=11\));
**c,d,** auto-FMT recipients (\(n=14\)). Each facet shows longitudinal HRIC
alpha diversity calculated with `HRIC::SHalpha` using the common 6,647-feature
dictionary. Curves and open circles show observations for one patient, and the
sample count is reported in the facet heading. All facets share the same axes.
Grey shading denotes the pre-HSCT period, and the black dashed vertical line
marks allo-HSCT infusion (day 0). Grey dotted lines mark neutrophil
engraftment; purple and orange dotted lines mark randomization without FMT and
auto-FMT/randomization, respectively. Diamonds identify stools collected on
the index day. Horizontal dashed lines show the earliest observed personal
pre-HSCT alpha diversity, and horizontal dotted lines show 80% of that
reference. Sustained 80% recovery was defined descriptively as the first
post-index crossing that was confirmed at the next observed sampling day
within event days 1-60. Panels are ordered by participant identifier within
each trial arm.
